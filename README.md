### Repair PDF – Fix Corrupted or Damaged PDF Files

The Repair PDF tool on PDFForce is designed to recover PDF files that fail to open, display incorrectly, or trigger errors. PDFs can become corrupted during downloads, transfers, storage, or software crashes, making important documents inaccessible.

PDFForce’s Repair PDF tool analyzes damaged files and rebuilds their internal structure to restore usability whenever possible. This helps recover content without recreating documents from scratch or losing original formatting.

Repair PDF is especially useful for contracts, invoices, scanned records, and archived documents where re-creation is time-consuming or impossible. Instead of discarding damaged files, users can attempt repair quickly and safely.

Repair PDF is often used alongside 
[Merge PDF](https://pdfforce.com/tools/merge) after recovery, 
[Compress PDF](https://pdfforce.com/tools/compress) to optimize repaired files, and 
[Protect PDF](https://pdfforce.com/tools/protect) to secure restored documents before sharing.

For scanned or archived files, users may apply 
[OCR PDF](https://pdfforce.com/tools/ocr) after repair to ensure content is searchable.

Repair PDF on PDFForce provides a practical solution for recovering access to important documents and keeping workflows moving.
